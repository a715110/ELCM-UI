Critical Dates

Dates written in contracts become tasks in ECWS ahead of time, so nobody has to remember them.

How it works

Each morning, and after each Apply, ELCM looks at the dates of active contract records. When a date is within its lead time, one task is requested in ECWS, due on that date.
Critical dates ECWS tasks Daily check Today
Who gets the task: the person in charge of the package that supplied the date, otherwise the approver with the fewest open tasks. A task is closed when a later Apply replaces the date or the record is no longer active.
